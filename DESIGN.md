# Nest Design System & Visuals

## Visual Direction
- **Typography:** Fraunces (serif for numbers and editorial headers) + DM Sans (clean geometric sans-serif).
- **Palette:** Deep forest ink (`#18251f`), soft sage/paper canvas (`#f8f9f7`), moss green positive accents (`#37745f`), warm amber warnings (`#a56c1d`), and muted terracotta red (`#a9433b`).
- **Cards & Structure:** Subtle 1px borders (`#dde4df`), rounded 14px corners, clean horizontal breathing room.
- **Interactions:**
  - Auto-advancing recommendation carousel with dot navigation and manual arrows.
  - Live allocation slider for HDFC Bank with instant calculation of downside reduction.
  - Clickable stock rows opening a slide-in thesis detail drawer.
