# Nest | Portfolio Intelligence

## Product Overview
Nest is an editorial, calm portfolio analysis dashboard designed for retail equity investors. It avoids financial terminal clutter, jargon, and excessive charts in favor of human, narrative-driven clarity.

## Key Product Sections
- **Hero & Returns:** Current value (₹17.8L), total return (+₹2.6L / +17.1%), and NIFTY 50 benchmark comparison (+14.8%).
- **Performance Chart:** 1Y comparative trendline with tabs (1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, 3Y, All).
- **Portfolio Health:** 4 pillars (Portfolio spread, Investment quality, Downside protection, Valuation safety).
- **What Stands Out:** Single-stock concentration callout (HDFC Bank: 43% of total gains).
- **Portfolio Fit:** Goal, risk preference, time horizon alignment score (82/100 Good Fit).
- **Recommendation Carousel:** Auto-rotating recommendations with visual allocation comparisons and impact trade-offs.
- **Interactive Simulation:** Live allocation slider for HDFC Bank showing downside reduction.
- **Portfolio Progress:** Multi-sync timeline and progress metrics.
- **Portfolio Pulse:** Activity updates and alerts.
- **Holdings Table & Detail Modal:** Overview of all holdings with click-to-view stock thesis modal.
